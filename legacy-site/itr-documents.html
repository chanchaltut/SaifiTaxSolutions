DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

ITR Filing ke Liye
Kaunse Documents Chahiye?

Stop scrambling for paperwork at the last minute! Having the right documents ready is 80% of the ITR filing battle. Here is your definitive, interactive checklist based on your exact income profile.

01

The Non-Negotiables (Base KYC)

Before jumping into your specific income type, make sure you have these three fundamental documents ready. These are required for every single taxpayer in India.

PAN Card

Must be active and linked.

Aadhaar Card

Mandatory to verify the return.

Bank Details

Pre-validated account for refunds.

02

Profile-Specific Checklists

Select your primary source of income below to see the exact documents you need to hand over to your tax consultant.

If you're a Salaried Person

Form 16 (Part A & B)Provided by your employer showing TDS and salary breakdown.
Form 26AS & AISCrucial to verify that your employer actually deposited your TDS.
Investment Proofs (80C etc.)LIC, PPF, ELSS statements if you opted for the Old Tax Regime.
Rent ReceiptsRequired to claim HRA (House Rent Allowance) exemption.

If you're a Business Owner (MSME/Trader)

Sales & Purchase DetailsTotal aggregate turnover and input costs for the financial year.
Bank StatementsFrom April 1 to March 31 (for all current and savings accounts).
GST Returns DataMonthly/Quarterly GSTR-1 and 3B details to match with income.
Books of Accounts / Previous ITRP&L, Balance Sheet (if audited), or previous year's business ITR.

If you're a Freelancer / Consultant

Invoices RaisedSummary of all professional invoices issued during the year.
Form 26AS (Very Important)To check the 10% TDS deducted by clients under section 194J.
FIRC / PayPal StatementsIf you have foreign clients, these act as proof of inward remittance.
Business ExpensesInternet bills, software subscriptions, and laptop depreciation bills.
03

Other Income Sources (Don't Miss These!)

Tax notices often arrive because people forget to declare secondary income. Check if any of these apply to you:

Capital Gains (Stocks & Crypto)

If you trade in stocks, mutual funds, or Crypto, you must download the Tax P&L Statement from your broker (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, etc.).

Rental Income & Property Sale

Keep your Rent Agreement, Municipal Tax receipts, and Home Loan Interest Certificate (to claim Section 24 deduction). For property sales, keep the Sale Deed handy.

Bank Interest & Foreign Assets

Interest certificates for your FDs and Savings accounts. If you work in an MNC and get foreign shares (RSUs/ESPPs) or have foreign bank accounts, this must be declared in schedule FA.
